Your single-minded determination kicks in. With conviction, you round the clearing, revisiting the brambles, pushing through the thrushes -

You must find these blossoms, everyone is expecting this of you. You don't care much about what people think of you, necessarily. But you've always outperformed, always fought the hardest. Was it always because of your need to protect others? Or was it more selfish than that, did you always need to be the best? Is being the best what will get you to the queen's side? Is that why you push others away? Is it because they only get in the way?


...


You're gritting your teeth again. Thankfully the pain is a bit sharp enough to snap you out of the train of thought. You don't unpack it; you dismiss it again. A bit of rage lingers, you wish you could punch something to get it out, but you manage to push that feeling down too.
