About our Stories




This narrative is meant to be a character exploration challenge with emphasis on exploring cultural narratives. As always, we draw inspiration from classical literature as they can present lessons that take on new meaning as society evolves. The beauty of classical literature tradition is that is becomes a part of the world. The stories transcend their original design. We hope to encapsulate the traditions that made these stories so special to us while also allowing these stories a chance to teach us new lessons. We hope that by using a Choose Your Own Adventure Format will allow our audience to engage with these narratives in a more literal way.

Vel'as is a pallid elf born into the fey who has wholly dedicated herself to her mission to protect her village and be a beacon of strength. The fey she lives in is stuck in perpetual twilight and summer as the Queen of Night and Magic and the summer elves usurped the Moonlit King, imprisoning him, and dismantling his court. Previously, the fey's seasons would wax and wane based on which court was in power, allowing for balance that extended to the fey's connection to the material plane. The pallid elves particularly derive their power from their connection to Sehanine, a moon Goddess of the material realm. Since the moon no longer rises, prayers have fallen on deaf ears and the pallid elves weaken. To supplement the decline, Queen Nicnevin came forward with a gift of transformative hemocraft. In exchange for secrets of the blood rite and magic, the strongest few of those who completed the pact would serve in her secret army while the others would remain to continue serving the elves as protectors. Out of desperation, they accepted. Vel'as' only focus has ever been to complete the task and be at the queen's side. She's only ever engaged in tasks alone and has a hard time with expressing herself or letting anyone in. At least, until she has no choice but to ask for help.
