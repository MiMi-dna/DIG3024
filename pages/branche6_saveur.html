You're bleeding a bit more than expected. Now that the adrenaline has worn off, you're forced to sit until the world stops spinning. You sit in silence often, but this is the first time in a while that thoughts start to creep in… You're frustrated knowing that any progress you've made has been moot. You also know that you need to report this incident to the Kwasvirkin, blood hunter soldiers whose job is to hunt creatures like these. The whole reason you're out here is to complete the ritual and climb rank. You need to be best of these members to be selected by Nicnevin, the queen of witches. Just the thought reminds you of the pulverized petals scattered around you. The pit in your stomach grows a bit. You must look like a mess. You spare a moment to consider how disappointed they might be. You're supposed to be the top performer in your group, the one who bares every burden silently and efficiently. But here you are, broken…

Before you dwell on this moment any longer, you dismiss it. You push out any feeling of failure, any hope that you might not look like an idiot. None of that matters. There is no “if,” only “when.” Failure is not an option. You push the pit in your stomach further down until you can't feel it anymore.



Once the world is still, you get up and manage to make your way back home. You don't remember what looks you were given when returning to the village and making your report in such a state. You simply focus on treating your wounds and resting until it's time to go on another venture. After some time, you are ready to head back to the forest.
