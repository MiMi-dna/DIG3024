You crouch low and push the gently bushes aside to get close enough to hear.



“For someone who talks so much, he's been keeping awfully quiet about where he's finding all those blooms. It's insane how many dropped when he ran into you back at the barracks!”


You remember this altercation. Recently, there was commotion in the courtyard that involved these guys. But you strictly remember the "Ulf'ren" guy being shoulder checked. That wasn't an accident. You can't remember if someone ended stepping in… Did you just leave? Does it matter?

“Does he really talk so much? I thought he was always the quiet type.”

“…Only if he's into something.”

“Maybe he's not into this whole flower thing or ritual, then?”

“Does it matter? We just need to find him to whatever he's managed to collect. If he doesn't have anything, we'll get him to tell us where to get 'em. One way or another, we're going to get this &%@! Over with…”



You manage to remain unnoticed, following them for a bit longer. You begin to wonder why you've decided to tail them for so long, it feels like a waste of time. Was this just curiosity. Just as you decide to turn away, one of them yells out.
You see that one of them manageable to grab a sizeable stone and is beginning to take aim. As you peer further out, you realize that there's another with dark hair. His back is still turned. It seems as if he didn't hear the call out!



  • You're disappointed.
    How could someone so unaware and unprepared get this far.
    It's not your place to step in; you all need to learn how to defend yourselves…

  • This is messed up; you leap into action!
