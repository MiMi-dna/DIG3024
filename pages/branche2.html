You manage to eventually spot another clearing. A warm mist clings at ankle height against your bare feet. Your training has made your steps soft but decisive. Even with the heaviness of the great axe at your back, you are able to deftly maneuver through the underbrush until you are free of difficult terrain. You found the last bloom hiding underneath some foliage as if it were being guarded by the thorned bramble around it. You start looking at the edges of the clearing, avoiding as many knicks as you can. If time existed here in a traditional sense, you'd probably had been at it for hours with no luck. It might be time to wrap things up and move on.

You tense and freeze, someone's near...

You think you hear something. Instinctively, you dodge into a thicket as you hear voices approaching. You smile slightly at the excess precaution but spare no thought at what may have inspired you to hide. You press against the rough bark of a tree, the pain of it brushing against the bare parts of your shoulders snaps you back into the moment. It sounds like the voices are arguing…



  • As the voices grow louder, you think you recognize them. They may be apart of your training group.
    You decide to see what this commotion is all about.

  • You don't need this distraction right now.
    The way you came lies ahead of you, it's an easy escape.
