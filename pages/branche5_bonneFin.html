“Wait really? You'll train with me? That would be great!”


“Yeah, it's all I really have for a trade. I am actually surprised that you accepted so easily.”


“…Oh wait... You thought this was supposed to be a trade? Are you always this serious?”


You don't know how to respond to this. Everything is transactional… You can't really tell if he's messing with you, so you assume that the terms of your agreement are still in effect. You shrug in response.
This causes UIf'ren to give a bellowing deep belly laugh followed by…

“Ow-ow-ow, okay, okay, sorry, where were we? Oh yeah!”

He touches behind his ear gently, still grinning widely as he starts to look about. Something catches his eye.

“Okay, so this is what you need to look for…”



He talks… so much…



But you try to stay focused as he tries to explain how the earth should feel and smell. He lists off a variety of plants that grow around the blooms. It's enough to make your head spin.
You are very bad at it, but he remains completely engaged in showing and working with you. It's all a bit overwhelming but you stick with him. He's so... passionate about this...
After some time, you manage to find your own blooms, enough for the ritual. After you both decide to retire, he doesn't seem to leave you. And for the first time, you don't seem to mind someone else's presence.



Back at the barracks, you notice some stares and some whispers. You must look like an odd pair, especially since your resting face tends to dissuade anyone from talking to you in general. In the mess, you start to clean off and prepare the blooms you collected. He pulls out some herbs that he had stored in his pouch and begins to make tea. You can get used to this…
