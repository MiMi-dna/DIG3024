Picking up the pace, mindlessly you search. Any intrusive thought gets immediately pushed away. You only have space to dedicate every action or thought into finding this bloom. Finally! It looks like you may have actually caught a glimpse of a bolbaena. You hurry forward and part the bramble bush. As you do, a hot anger takes hold.


You taste blood.



It's enough to shock you back into the moment as your lip throbs. There's no change in your countenance, it's as calm as every but your insides are twisted as you look upon the scattered petals upon the ground. The reddish glow you saw came from the last glows of the crushed bulbaena flower.

But how is that possible? You are the only one here…

Seething, you don't give the dying flower another thought. It's time to go. But the rage remains.
You allow it to remain this time as it helps push down the thought that you may have been the one to get in your own way...



  • You accept defeat only for today.
    Heading home, you push your tongue against your sore lip.
    The numb pain is enough of a distraction for now...
