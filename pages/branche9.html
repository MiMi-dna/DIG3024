The trees have a sense of humor... Growing more frustrated, you decide to turn back and do at least another full round insted of treading further into the deepest parts of the forest. The last thing you need is to run into a valley escapee. Or worse... a nest of pixies... Normally when you start feeling this way, taking it out on a training dummy is the best way to let it out. But your a bit a way's from home and you know better to go hacking at a tree in the fey.

You turn around to follow the trail you started on. But as you do, it now feels like the trees are attempting to bar your path. They don't move per se. But it's as if the realm pushes things around you.
You know well enough that it may be a sign.



  • You know better than to run around in circles. You decide to go home to recollect yourself, try again after you get some rest...

  • Despite the wood attempting to work against you, you don't take defeat easily. You are determined to find something here.

  • You don't take to defeat easily and this is no exception. You decide on trying another area after all.
